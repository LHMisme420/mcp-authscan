---
name: Billing ownership
description: Why new verified accounts are separate from historical name-only workspaces
---

New authenticated billing accounts must not automatically inherit historical
findings by matching an organization name or an independently allocated account ID.
Keep historical data intact and separate until ownership is verified and an
explicit import or association is approved.

**Why:** The old name-only login did not establish who owned an organization.
Matching names or coincident IDs would disclose another organization's findings
and could attach someone else's paid subscription.

**How to apply:** Use verified identity for account and customer ownership.
Any future legacy-history recovery needs explicit ownership approval, not
automatic claiming by name.
