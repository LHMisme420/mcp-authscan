VATA Verification-First Methodology

Core Principles

  1. Verify Before Publishing: All claims must be verified against live scanning data
  2. Attribution Required: Findings must include specific server and endpoint targets for independent verification
  3. Reproducibility: Results must be reproducible on independent re-scan
  4. Transparency: Methodology and limitations published alongside results
  5. Honest Reporting: Findings are reported at the level they can be verified - no inferences
